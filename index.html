About me

I’m a Finnish developer. I build Discord bots, websites, and tools, mostly using Codex and Antigravity.

Most of my projects start with something I wish were easier in the communities I’m part of. With RobloTracker, that means making Discord alerts easier to manage and figuring out why an alert arrived late.
